# SOP: Opening a Cashier Shift

## Before opening the shift
1. Arrive 15 minutes before the shift starts, in full uniform with your name tag.
2. Turn on the cashier tablet, the receipt printer, and the card (EDC) terminal.
3. Make sure the internet connection is stable. Check that the sync icon in Hitachi POS is green.
4. Make sure there is enough receipt paper. Keep one spare roll next to the cashier.

## Opening the shift in the app
1. Sign in to Hitachi POS with your own cashier account.
2. Open the **Shift** menu and tap **Open Shift**.
3. Count the starting cash in the cash drawer and enter it in the **Opening Cash** field. The standard opening cash at Kopi Tepi is Rp500,000 in small notes.
4. Tap **Start**. Transactions can only be created after the shift is open.

## Required checks
- Print a test receipt from **Settings > Devices > Printer > Test Print**.
- Make sure the static QRIS code at the counter can be scanned, and that a dynamic QRIS code appears in a test.
- Check the main ingredients (milk, coffee beans, ice) and tell the barista if anything is running low.

## If the previous shift is still open
The app will not open a new shift while the previous one is still open. Ask the previous cashier or the owner to close it first. See the SOP for closing a shift.
