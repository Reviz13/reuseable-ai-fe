# Customer FAQ

## Is there Wi-Fi?
Yes. The network is KopiTepi-Guest, and the password is printed at the bottom of the receipt.

## Can I pay with QRIS or a card?
Yes. Kopi Tepi accepts cash, QRIS from any payment app, and debit and credit cards.

## Do prices include tax?
No. Menu prices do not include the 11% VAT, which is added at payment.

## Is there a caffeine-free drink?
Hot Chocolate is caffeine-free. Matcha Latte, Lemon Tea, and Iced Teh Tarik contain a little caffeine from tea.

## Is there non-dairy milk?
Yes, oat milk can replace cow's milk in any milk-based drink.

## Can I order for an event?
Yes. Orders of more than 20 cups should be placed at least one day in advance through the cashier or the outlet WhatsApp.

## Can I reserve a table?
Kopi Tepi does not take table reservations except for events of at least 15 people. Contact the owner to arrange it.

## What if my order is wrong?
Tell the cashier. The order will be replaced or refunded under the refund SOP.

## Can I bring my pet?
Yes, on the outdoor terrace.
