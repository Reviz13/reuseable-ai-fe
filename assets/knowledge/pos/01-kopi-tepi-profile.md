# Kopi Tepi Outlet Profile

Note: Kopi Tepi is the sample outlet for the Hitachi POS demo. The outlet details below are sample data.

## About Kopi Tepi
Kopi Tepi is a small coffee shop serving milk coffee, manual brew coffee, non-coffee drinks, and light meals. Its outlet code in Hitachi POS is kopi-tepi-01.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 07:00 to 22:00.
- Saturday and Sunday: 08:00 to 23:00.
- Public holidays: 09:00 to 21:00.
- Last order is taken 30 minutes before closing.

## Work shifts
- Morning shift: 07:00 to 15:00.
- Evening shift: 14:30 to 22:30 (23:30 on weekends).
- The 30-minute overlap is used for the handover between cashiers.

## Facilities
- Free Wi-Fi for customers. Network name: KopiTepi-Guest. The password is printed at the bottom of the receipt.
- Power outlets at the bar counter and the long table by the window.
- A separate smoking area on the outdoor terrace. The indoor area is smoke-free.
- Pets are welcome on the terrace.
- High chairs for babies and a toilet are available.

## Order types
- Dine in and take away.
- Delivery through ride-hailing apps. Prices in delivery apps may differ from prices at the cashier.
- Large orders (more than 20 cups) should be placed at least one day in advance.

## Contact
Customer questions, partnership requests, and complaints can be raised with the cashier or sent to the owner through the outlet WhatsApp number printed on the receipt.
