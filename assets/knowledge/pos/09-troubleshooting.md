# Troubleshooting Devices and the App

## The printer does not print
1. Make sure the printer is on and the indicator light is green.
2. Check the thermal paper: the roll must face the print head with the shiny side.
3. In the app, open **Settings > Devices > Printer** and tap **Test Print**.

## The Bluetooth printer disconnected
Turn the tablet's Bluetooth off and on again, then pair the printer again from **Settings > Devices > Find Printer**.

## Faded printing
The thermal paper may be expired or the print head may be dirty. Clean the print head with a cotton bud and isopropyl alcohol, then replace the paper.

## QRIS payment failed or pending
1. Ask the customer to check their balance and their phone's internet connection.
2. Wait up to 1 minute, then check the payment status in the app.
3. If the customer's balance was deducted but the status is failed, note the reference number from the customer's payment proof and report it to the owner. Never accept a double payment.
4. Offer another payment method if QRIS keeps failing.

## The internet is down
The app can still record cash transactions while offline and syncs them when the internet returns. QRIS and card payments cannot be processed without internet. Use the outlet phone's backup hotspot if available.

## The cash drawer does not open
The cash drawer opens automatically after a cash payment. If it does not, check the cable between the drawer and the printer. In an emergency, use the manual key kept by the owner.

## The app is slow or frozen
Close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, restart the tablet. Paid transactions are kept.

## The tablet turned off during a transaction
Turn it back on, sign in, and check the **Transactions** menu to confirm whether the last transaction was recorded before creating the order again.
