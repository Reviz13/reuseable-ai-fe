# Stock, COGS, and Consignment Items

## What COGS means
COGS (cost of goods sold, HPP in Indonesian) is the ingredient cost of making one product, such as coffee beans, milk, sugar, cups, and straws. Profit is sales minus COGS.

## How profit is calculated in Hitachi POS
- Sales: the total price of products sold (before VAT).
- COGS: the sum of the COGS of every product sold.
- Profit: sales minus COGS.
- Margin: profit divided by sales, as a percentage.

Profit does not include other costs such as salaries, rent, electricity, or VAT.

## Products without COGS
Products without a COGS value are still counted in sales, but their cost is not included yet. Profit therefore looks higher than it really is. At Kopi Tepi, the product without COGS is the Consignment Layer Cake, because it is a consignment item from a small local business partner. The Dashboard and NEXUS show a note with the number of items sold without COGS.

## Consignment items
Consignment items are sold on behalf of a partner. Kopi Tepi receives a revenue share under the agreement. At the end of each week, the owner checks the number sold with the partner and pays the partner's share.

## Changing a product's price or COGS
Only the owner can change selling prices and COGS, through Hitachi POS Admin in the **Products** menu. Changes apply to the next transactions and do not change past transactions.

## Daily stock check
- The barista checks ingredients at every shift opening and closing.
- Coffee beans are stored in airtight containers and used within 4 weeks of the roast date.
- Fresh milk is kept in the fridge and used within 2 days of opening.
- Ingredients that are running low are written on the stock board and reported to the owner.
