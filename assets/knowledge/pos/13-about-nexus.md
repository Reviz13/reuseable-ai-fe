# About NEXUS, the Hitachi POS AI Assistant

## What NEXUS is
NEXUS is the AI assistant built into the Hitachi POS app. NEXUS helps cashiers and owners with questions about outlet operations and how to use the app, and helps owners check profit.

## What you can ask NEXUS
- Profit, sales, and COGS for today, yesterday, this week, last week, this month, last month, a specific date (for example "Profit on 30 September?"), or a date range (for example "Sales from 1 to 15 October?"). Owner only, up to 92 days at a time, and not for future dates.
- Kopi Tepi menu, prices, allergens, and promotions.
- SOPs for opening and closing a shift, payments, refunds, and voids.
- The membership and points program.
- Troubleshooting the printer, QRIS, internet, and cash drawer.
- Staff rules, service standards, and cleanliness.
- How to use the Cashier and Dashboard pages.

## NEXUS limits
- NEXUS only answers from the knowledge documents uploaded by the admin. If the information is not there, NEXUS says it could not find it.
- Profit figures always come directly from the POS system and are never guessed.
- Cashiers cannot see profit. NEXUS refuses profit questions from cashier accounts.
- NEXUS can only read data. It cannot change transactions, prices, or stock.

## How to use NEXUS
- Tap the NEXUS avatar in the corner of the screen, or the **Ask NEXUS** button at the top.
- Use the quick suggestion buttons for common questions.
- Give an answer a thumbs up or thumbs down to help the admin improve the knowledge.

## Adding knowledge to NEXUS
Admins can add documents or FAQs in Admin Studio under Knowledge. Questions NEXUS could not answer appear under Insights as ideas for new documents.
