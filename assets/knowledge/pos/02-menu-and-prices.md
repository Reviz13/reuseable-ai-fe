# Kopi Tepi Menu and Prices

All prices below match the menu on the Hitachi POS cashier screen. Prices do not include the 11% VAT, which is added automatically at payment.

## Coffee
- Tepi Iced Milk Coffee, Rp24,000. The signature drink: espresso, fresh milk, and palm sugar, served iced. Can be made less sweet.
- Americano, Rp22,000. Espresso with hot or cold water, no milk and no sugar.
- Cappuccino, Rp28,000. Espresso with steamed milk and thick milk foam. Available hot or iced.
- Tubruk Coffee, Rp15,000. Coarsely ground robusta brewed directly with hot water, served with sugar on the side.
- V60 Single Origin, Rp35,000. Manual pour-over with a single origin bean that changes every week. Ask the barista for this week's bean.

## Non-coffee
- Matcha Latte, Rp30,000. Japanese matcha with fresh milk, hot or iced.
- Hot Chocolate, Rp26,000. Premium cocoa with steamed milk. Can be served iced.
- Iced Teh Tarik, Rp18,000. Black tea with sweetened condensed milk, pulled until frothy.
- Lemon Tea, Rp16,000. Jasmine tea with fresh lemon juice, hot or iced.

## Food
- Kaya Toast, Rp20,000. Thick toast with kaya (coconut egg jam) and butter.
- Butter Croissant, Rp25,000. Croissant warmed in the oven before serving.
- Cheese Banana Fritters, Rp18,000. Fried banana topped with grated cheese and condensed milk.
- Village Fried Rice, Rp32,000. Fried rice with a fried egg, crackers, and pickles. Spice level can be adjusted.
- Consignment Layer Cake, Rp12,000. Layer cake from a small local business partner (a consignment item).

## Add-ons and customization
- An extra espresso shot, oat milk, or syrup (vanilla, caramel, hazelnut) can be requested from the barista. The extra is recorded as an order note.
- Sugar level: normal, less sweet, or no sugar.
- Ice: normal, less ice, or no ice.

## Allergen information
- Contains milk: Tepi Iced Milk Coffee, Cappuccino, Matcha Latte, Hot Chocolate, Iced Teh Tarik, Butter Croissant, Cheese Banana Fritters, Kaya Toast.
- Contains gluten: Kaya Toast, Butter Croissant, Cheese Banana Fritters (batter), Consignment Layer Cake.
- Contains egg: Village Fried Rice, Butter Croissant, Kaya Toast, Consignment Layer Cake.
- Customers with allergies should tell the cashier before ordering. Oat milk is available instead of cow's milk.
