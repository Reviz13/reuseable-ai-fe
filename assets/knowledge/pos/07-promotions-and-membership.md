# Promotions and Membership

## Registering a member
Open **Customers > Add Member** and enter the name and phone number. Membership is free.

## Member points
- Members earn 1 point for every Rp10,000 spent.
- Every 50 points can be exchanged for one free regular-size drink.
- Points are valid for 12 months from the day they are earned.

## Member discount
During a transaction, tap **Customers**, search for the member's phone number, and the 10% member discount is applied automatically to drinks.

## Regular promotions at Kopi Tepi
- Happy Hour: every day from 14:00 to 16:00, buy two Tepi Iced Milk Coffee and get 20% off the second cup.
- Breakfast Bundle: from 07:00 to 10:00, Tubruk Coffee and Kaya Toast are sold as a bundle.
- Member birthday: on their birthday, a member gets one free drink by showing their ID card.

## Promotion rules
- Promotions cannot be combined unless stated otherwise.
- The member discount does not apply to the Consignment Layer Cake because it is a consignment item.
- Promotions do not apply to orders through delivery apps.
