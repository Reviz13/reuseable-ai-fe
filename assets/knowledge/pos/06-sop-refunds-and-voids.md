# SOP: Refunds and Voids

## Void versus refund
- Void: cancels an order that has not been paid. A cashier can void without the owner's approval.
- Refund: returns money for a paid transaction. It always needs the owner's approval.

## When a refund is allowed
Refunds are only for transactions from the same day, before the shift is closed. Transactions from earlier days must be sent to the owner with a correction form.

## Accepted refund reasons
- The order was entered or made incorrectly.
- The product turned out to be unavailable after payment.
- The product did not meet quality standards (for example a spilled drink or cold food).

## How to refund
1. Open the **Transactions** menu and find the receipt number or order number.
2. Select the transaction and tap **Refund**.
3. Choose the items being returned and the refund reason.
4. Ask the owner to enter the **owner PIN** to approve.
5. Return the money with the original payment method: cash from the drawer, QRIS or card through the cancellation process.
6. Print the refund receipt and keep it with the original receipt.

## Partial refunds
For a partial refund, select only the items being returned. Promotional discounts are recalculated automatically.

## Replacing a product
If the customer agrees, a faulty product can be replaced with the same product without a refund. Record the replacement in the order note.
