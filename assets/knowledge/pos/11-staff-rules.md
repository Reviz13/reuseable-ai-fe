# Staff Rules and Service Standards

## Attendance
- Clock in and out through the app.
- If you will be more than 15 minutes late, tell the owner before your shift starts.
- Shift swaps must be approved by the owner at least one day in advance.

## Uniform and appearance
Wear the Kopi Tepi uniform shirt, an apron, and your name tag. Tie back long hair. Keep nails short and clean.

## Service standards
1. Greet a customer within 5 seconds of them reaching the cashier.
2. Repeat the order back to the customer before tapping Pay.
3. Offer the membership program to customers who are not members yet.
4. Thank the customer and call out the order number when handing over the order.
5. The target time to serve a drink is 5 minutes when the queue is normal.

## Cleanliness and food safety
- Wash your hands before preparing orders and after handling money.
- Wipe customer tables as soon as they are free.
- Clean the espresso machine and steam wand at the end of every shift.
- Use separate cutting boards for cooked food and raw ingredients.

## Handling customer complaints
Listen to the end, apologize, offer a replacement or a refund under the SOP, then record the complaint and report it to the owner at the end of the shift.

## Data confidentiality
Cashiers must not share sales, profit, or member data with anyone. Profit information is for the owner only.
