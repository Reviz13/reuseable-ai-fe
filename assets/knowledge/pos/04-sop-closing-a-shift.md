# SOP: Closing a Cashier Shift

## Before closing the shift
- Make sure no order is still pending. Complete or cancel it first.
- Make sure every QRIS and card payment shows as successful.

## Closing the shift in the app
1. Open the **Shift** menu and tap **Close Shift**.
2. Count the physical cash in the drawer and enter it in the **Actual Cash** field.
3. The app shows the difference between the actual cash and the system cash.
4. If the difference is more than Rp10,000, write an explanation and report it to the owner.
5. Print the **Shift Report** and hand it to the next cashier or to the owner.

## Cash deposit
Cash above the opening amount goes into a deposit envelope marked with the date, the cashier's name, and the amount, and is stored in the safe. The opening cash stays in the drawer for the next shift.

## Handover
The next cashier opens a new shift with the opening cash shown in the previous shift report.
