# SOP: Transactions and Payments

## Creating an order on the Cashier screen
1. Open the **Cashier** page. Use the category tabs (All, Coffee, Non-coffee, Food) or the **Search menu** box to find a product.
2. Tap a product card to add it to the cart. Each tap adds one. The number in the corner of the card shows how many are in the cart.
3. In the **Order** panel, change quantities with the minus and plus buttons, or tap **Remove** to take an item out.
4. The subtotal, 11% VAT, and total are calculated automatically.

## Cash payment
1. Choose **Cash**.
2. Enter the cash received, or tap a quick button: **Exact amount**, Rp50,000, Rp100,000, or Rp200,000.
3. The app shows the change. If the cash is not enough, the Pay button is disabled and "Short by" is shown.
4. Tap **Pay**, hand over the change, then give the receipt.

## QRIS payment
1. Choose **QRIS** and tap **Pay**.
2. Ask the customer to scan the QR code and show the successful payment on their phone.
3. Do not hand over the order before the payment is successful. If it fails, see the troubleshooting guide for QRIS.

## Debit or credit card payment
Use the card (EDC) terminal. Enter the total shown on screen, ask the customer to enter their PIN, and keep the EDC slip with the receipt.

## After payment
A **Payment successful** screen shows the order number, items, total, and change. Tap **New transaction** to serve the next customer.

## Tax
VAT is 11% of the subtotal, rounded to the nearest rupiah. Menu prices do not include VAT.

## Split bills
For a bill paid separately, create one order per person. Do not split one receipt by hand.

## Discounts
Discounts may only be given under an active promotion or with the owner's approval. See the Promotions and Membership guide.
